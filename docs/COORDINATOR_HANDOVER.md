# Coordinator Handover SOP

Open **Priority Queue** at 09:00 and handle rank **#1** first — uncovered elder or child care gaps outrank everything else. Before dialling, open the issue and check two panels: the **Message Timeline** (for preferences like "WhatsApp, don't call") and the **Booking Register** (sole authority on arrivals, substitutes, refund approvals, and payout dates). Never promise an ETA, substitute, refund approval, or quote missing from the register.

If priority, owner, or next action looks wrong, submit a decision in **Coordinator Review** (`change_priority`, `change_department`, `correct_action`, or `edit_draft`) with a short reason so the queue re-ranks immediately. Drafts are never auto-sent — copy an approved draft manually or hold contact. Escalate uncertain booking identities via **Request More Information** and close verified arrivals via **Resolve**.
